# 1. Basic Lesson

## 1.1 Getting Ready

### 1.1.1 Lesson 1 Introduction to JetHexa

#### 1.1.1.1 Product Description



JetHexa is an open source hexapod robot based on Robot Operating System (ROS). It is armed with high-performance hardware, such as NVIDIA Jetson Nano, intelligent serial bus servos, Lidar and HD camera/ 3D depth camera, which can implement robot motion control, mapping and navigation, tracking and obstacle avoidance, custom prowling, human feature recognition, somatosensory interaction and other functions. Adopted novel inverse kinematics algorithm, supporting tripod and ripple gaits and with highly configurable body posture, height and speed, JetHexa will bring user ultimate using experience.



JetHexa not only serves as an advanced platform for user to learn and verify hexapod movement, but also provides solutions for ROS development. To help user embark on a new journey of ROS hexapod robotic world, ample ROS and robot learning materials and tutorials are provided.

### 1.1.2 Lesson 2 Prepare for Starting JetHexa

#### 1.1.2.1 Charging and Application Notice



JetHexa is powered by 11.V Lipo battery. For delivery safety, the battery will not be fully charged, so please use the charger included in the package to charge the battery when you receive the product.



It takes 1 hour to fully charge the battery. Pay attention please charge the robot in time when the voltage is below 9.6V for better robot performance.



1. Push the switch of the exapnsion board to “**OFF**” side.



<img src="../_static/media/chapter_1/section_2/media/image2.png" class="common_img" style="width:800px;" />



2. Remove these two screws on the bottom with screwdriver.



<img src="../_static/media/chapter_1/section_2/media/image3.png" class="common_img" style="width:800px;" />



3. Take out the battery, and connect the wire as pictured. (Red to red and black to black)



<img src="../_static/media/chapter_1/section_2/media/image4.png" class="common_img" style="width:800px;" />



4. Having connected the wire, put the battery back to its original place, and install the cover and fix it.



<img src="../_static/media/chapter_1/section_2/media/image5.png" class="common_img" style="width:800px;" />



5. Insert the charger to the DC port on Jetson Nano expansion board to charge the robot.



<img src="../_static/media/chapter_1/section_2/media/image6.png" class="common_img" style="width:800px;" />



When the red light is on, the robot is being charged, and it is fully charged when green light turns on.



#### 1.1.2.2 **Using Instruction of Lipo Battery**



1. Before charging and using the robot, please connect the battery wire. And red wire should connect to red wire, and black to black.



2. Please use the charger comes with JetHexa to charge.



3. When the battery charger is connected to the battery without being plugged into an AC power outlet, the charger LED indicator will turn green.



4. When the charger is connected to battery and AC power outlet, the indicator will turn red representing that the battery is being charged. When the red indicator light turns green, the battery is fully charged. After charging, remove the charging cable timely to prevent battery from damage caused by overcharging.



5. If the battery butted line is not connected to the controller, please do not directly connect to the battery to prevent short circuit caused by the contact of the positive and negative electrode.



6. If the robot is not used for a long time, please fully charge the robot and then disconnect the line connecting the robot and the battery. Do not fiercely pull the line to avoid the damage to the interface.



7. Keep the battery far away from high temperature and all kinds of liquids to prevent overheating, fire or damp, which results in function decline and short service life.

### 1.1.3 Lesson 3 Start JetHexa

**Notice:**



**1. To ensure robot’s normal working, please fully charge the robot when its voltage is below 9.6V first, then continue your operation.**



**2. Please do not put the robot on uneven surface when starting the robot**



**3. When the robot is working, please keep your face and eyes from the robot to avoid injury.**



**4. Please do not put your finger within the motion range of the robot’s joints when robot is moving, otherwise the joints will be damaged.**



**5. Please watch out for dropping the robot from a height level.**



**6. Continuous long--time working may heat up the servo. And the servo needs to take a break and cannot back to work until it cools down.**



**7. Please get the servo change after long-time use or excessive use.**

### 1.2.2 Lesson 2 Color Threshold Setting

One color under different lights may deviate, which has impact on the realization of color recognition.



To tackle this problem, we need to adjust the color threshold on the APP. Take operation on Android device for example, and the instruction is also applicable to iOS device.



1. Start JetHexa, and connect it to the APP “**WonderAi**”.



2. Enter Mode Selection interface, and the click <img src="../_static/media/chapter_1/section_7/media/image2.png" class="inline-icon" style="width:70px;" /> to enter the color threshold setting interface.



3. Take adjusting red for example. Place the red block within the camera frame, and then select red.



<img src="../_static/media/chapter_1/section_7/media/image3.png" class="common_img" style="width:800px;" />



4. Click “**instruction**” to check how to make adjustment.



<img src="../_static/media/chapter_1/section_7/media/image4.png" class="common_img" style="width:800px;" />



<img src="../_static/media/chapter_1/section_7/media/image5.png" class="common_img" style="width:800px;" />



5. Set all the “ **\_min**” values as “**0**” and “**\_max”** values as “**255**”.



<img src="../_static/media/chapter_1/section_7/media/image6.png" class="common_img" style="width:800px;" />



6. According to Lab color model diagram, adjust L, A and B components to approach the target color zone.



<img src="../_static/media/chapter_1/section_7/media/image7.png" class="common_img" style="width:800px;" />



Red is around “**+a**” zone, hence A component needs to be increased, that is “**L_max**” value remains unchanged and “**L_min**” value needs to be increased until the red block at left turns white and other area turns black.



<img src="../_static/media/chapter_1/section_7/media/image8.png" class="common_img" style="width:800px;" />

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_1/section_7/media/image9.png" class="common_img" style="width:800px;" />
