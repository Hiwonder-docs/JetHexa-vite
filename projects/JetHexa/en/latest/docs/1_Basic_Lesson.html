1.1.1 Lesson 1 Introduction to JetHexa ​

1.1.1.1 Product Description ​

JetHexa is an open source hexapod robot based on Robot Operating System (ROS). It is armed with high-performance hardware, such as NVIDIA Jetson Nano, intelligent serial bus servos, Lidar and HD camera/ 3D depth camera, which can implement robot motion control, mapping and navigation, tracking and obstacle avoidance, custom prowling, human feature recognition, somatosensory interaction and other functions. Adopted novel inverse kinematics algorithm, supporting tripod and ripple gaits and with highly configurable body posture, height and speed, JetHexa will bring user ultimate using experience.

JetHexa not only serves as an advanced platform for user to learn and verify hexapod movement, but also provides solutions for ROS development. To help user embark on a new journey of ROS hexapod robotic world, ample ROS and robot learning materials and tutorials are provided.

1.1.2 Lesson 2 Prepare for Starting JetHexa ​

1.1.2.1 Charging and Application Notice ​

JetHexa is powered by 11.V Lipo battery. For delivery safety, the battery will not be fully charged, so please use the charger included in the package to charge the battery when you receive the product.

It takes 1 hour to fully charge the battery. Pay attention please charge the robot in time when the voltage is below 9.6V for better robot performance.

  1. Push the switch of the exapnsion board to “OFF” side.
  1. Remove these two screws on the bottom with screwdriver.
  1. Take out the battery, and connect the wire as pictured. (Red to red and black to black)
  1. Having connected the wire, put the battery back to its original place, and install the cover and fix it.
  1. Insert the charger to the DC port on Jetson Nano expansion board to charge the robot.

When the red light is on, the robot is being charged, and it is fully charged when green light turns on.

1.1.2.2 Using Instruction of Lipo Battery ​

  1. Before charging and using the robot, please connect the battery wire. And red wire should connect to red wire, and black to black.

  2. Please use the charger comes with JetHexa to charge.

  3. When the battery charger is connected to the battery without being plugged into an AC power outlet, the charger LED indicator will turn green.

  4. When the charger is connected to battery and AC power outlet, the indicator will turn red representing that the battery is being charged. When the red indicator light turns green, the battery is fully charged. After charging, remove the charging cable timely to prevent battery from damage caused by overcharging.

  5. If the battery butted line is not connected to the controller, please do not directly connect to the battery to prevent short circuit caused by the contact of the positive and negative electrode.

  6. If the robot is not used for a long time, please fully charge the robot and then disconnect the line connecting the robot and the battery. Do not fiercely pull the line to avoid the damage to the interface.

  7. Keep the battery far away from high temperature and all kinds of liquids to prevent overheating, fire or damp, which results in function decline and short service life.

1.1.3 Lesson 3 Start JetHexa ​

Notice:

1. To ensure robot’s normal working, please fully charge the robot when its voltage is below 9.6V first, then continue your operation.

2. Please do not put the robot on uneven surface when starting the robot

3. When the robot is working, please keep your face and eyes from the robot to avoid injury.

4. Please do not put your finger within the motion range of the robot’s joints when robot is moving, otherwise the joints will be damaged.

5. Please watch out for dropping the robot from a height level.

6. Continuous long--time working may heat up the servo. And the servo needs to take a break and cannot back to work until it cools down.

7. Please get the servo change after long-time use or excessive use.

1.1.3.1 Start JetHexa ​

Before booting up, please place JetHexa as pictured to avoid the damage caused by sudden force from servos. And do not fold the legs.

Push the switch of expansion board to “ON” side.

Then LED1 on expansion board will flash in blue. After a while, buzzer will beep and JetHexa will return to initial posture, which means that Ros completes configuration.

After successful start, the following data will be displayed on the dot matrix display module.

NameMeaning

The default network connection mode is AP direct connection mode. After successful start, JetHexa will generate a WiFi starting with “HW”. And the WiFi password is “hiwonder”. (For how to connect to Ubuntu desktop, please refer to the material in “7. Standard Motion Control Lesson->1. Basic Control Lesson->Lesson 1 Build Development Environment->1. Remote Tool Installation and Connection”)

1.2.1.1 APP Installation and Connection ​

In this lesson, you will learn how to download and install the "WonderAi" app, which allows you to control JetHexa. Once installed, you can use the app to remotely control JetHexa's various functions and features.

1. APP Installation

Note:

  • Turn on location and Wi-Fi before using the APP.

Method 1:

(1) Android user: the installation package can be found in Appendix and please transfer it to your phone for downloading.

(2) iOS user: you can download “WonderAi” in App Store.

Method 2:

Scan the below QR code to download the APP.

2. Boot up JetHexa

According to the file in “1. Basic Lesson->1. Getting Ready->Lesson 3 Start JetHexa”, start JetHexa first. After successful start, it will generate a WiFi starting with “HW”.

3. APP Connection

(1) Preparation

① Install “WonderAi” APP

② Start JetHexa following the instructions in “1. Basic Lesson->1. Getting Ready->Lesson 3 Start JetHexa”.

(2) Connection Mode Introduction

There are two network modes, including AP direct connection mode and STA LAN mode.

① AP direct connection mode: Jetson Nano generates a WiFi which can be connected by phones. This WiFi has no internet access.

② STA LAN mode: Jetson Nano actively connects to specific WiFi. You can access Internet in this mode.

The default connection mode is AP direct connection mode. Whether you choose AP direct connection mode or STA LAN mode, robot performs the same. However, it is recommended to experience robot games under Direct Connection Mode.

(3) Direct Connection (Must Read)

Take the Android APP for example. The instructions are also applicable to iOS device.

① Open “WonderAi” APP, then tap “Advanced -> JetHexa” in sequence.

② Select the corresponding version of JetHexa. Choose “JetHexa Standard”.

③ Click “+” button at the lower right corner, and then select “Direct Connection Mode”.

Note: if you choose “LAN mode”, you can connect the network referring to the file in “6. Jetson Nano and Jetson Nano Expansion Board->1. Jetson Nano Basic Lesson->Lesson 7 Jetson Nano Network Connection”.

④ Next, click “Go to connect device hotspots” to join the WiFi generated by JetHexa.

⑤ The device WiFi starts with “HW” and the password is “hiwonder”.

Note: for iOS user, please do not back to the APP until the WiFi icon appears above, otherwise JetHexa cannot be searched. If JetHexa cannot be searched, please click to refresh.

⑥ Return to the APP, and click the robotic icon to enter control interface.

If you are informed of “No Internet Connection. Whether to keep connection”, just select “Keep Connection”.

⑦ When the following window pops up, just click “cancel” and back to “Product Choice” interface to select the correct version.

⑧ The home interface of JetHexa is as the picture shows.

To learn about the instructions on each game, please refer to the file stored in “1. Basic Lesson->2. Quick User Experience->Lesson 1 APP Control->2. APP Control”.

(4) LAN Mode (Optional)

① Firstly, join 5G network, for example “Hiwomder_5G”. (The router supporting dual-frequency will distinguish the Wi-Fi name by default under the situation that 2.4 G and 5G are separated. For example, Wi-Fi “Hiwonder” is 2.4 frequency band while “Hiwonder_5G” is 5G frequency band)

② After connection, open WonderAi. Then click “Advanced->JeHexa” in sequence. (Take JetHexa standard kit for example)

③ Click “+” button at the lower right corner, then select LAN Mode.

④ Continue, enter the Wi-Fi password. Having entered the password, click “OK”. Please ensure the password you enter is correct.

⑤ Click “Go to connect device hotspots”.

⑥ Join the WiFi starting with HW, and input the password “hiwonder”. After connection, return back to the APP interface.

⑦ Then, WonderAi APP is connecting to the robot.

⑧ After a while, the robotic icon will show up. And at the same time, LED1 on the expansion board will light up.

⑨ By long pressing the robotic icon, you can check the IP address and ID.

⑩ Input this IP address in the search bar on remote desktop software. Then you can enter the robot system desktop. For how to operate, please refer to the file in “7. Standard Motion Control Lesson\1. Basic Control Lesson\ Lesson 1 Build Development Environment”.

⑪ If you want switch back to Direct Connect Mode, press KEY1 button on Jetson Nano expansion board until blue LED flashes, which means the current mode is Direct Connect Mode.

1.2.2 Lesson 2 Color Threshold Setting ​

One color under different lights may deviate, which has impact on the realization of color recognition.

To tackle this problem, we need to adjust the color threshold on the APP. Take operation on Android device for example, and the instruction is also applicable to iOS device.

  1. Start JetHexa, and connect it to the APP “WonderAi”.

  2. Enter Mode Selection interface, and the click to enter the color threshold setting interface.

  3. Take adjusting red for example. Place the red block within the camera frame, and then select red.

  1. Click “instruction” to check how to make adjustment.
  1. Set all the “ _min” values as “0” and “_max” values as “255”.
  1. According to Lab color model diagram, adjust L, A and B components to approach the target color zone.

Red is around “+a” zone, hence A component needs to be increased, that is “L_max” value remains unchanged and “L_min” value needs to be increased until the red block at left turns white and other area turns black.

  1. Based on the environment, modify “L” and “B” components. If it belongs to light red, increase “L_min”. Otherwise, decrease “L_max”. If it belongs to warm tone, increase “b_min”. Otherwise, decrease “b_max”.
  1. After adjustment, click “Save” to save the adjusted threshold.

1.2.3 Lesson 3 Wireless Handle Control ​

1.2.3.1 Preparation ​

1. Insert the handle receiver into any USB interface on JetHexa.

Note: if you have already inserted the handle receiver before booting up JetHexa, you can skip this step.

2. Please bring your own two AAA dry batteries. And insert them into the battery slot and please do not invert the positive and negative electrode.

1.2.3.2 Device Connection ​

1. Turn on JetHexa.

2. Turn on the switch on the handle. At this time, two LED lights (red and green) will flash simultaneously.

3. Please wait for a while. Then the robot will pair with the handle automatically. After successful pairing, the green light will keep lighting up.

If the handle doesn’t connect to the robot within 30s or there is no operation on the handle within 5 minutes after turning on, it will enter sleep mode. And you can press “START” to activate the handle.
